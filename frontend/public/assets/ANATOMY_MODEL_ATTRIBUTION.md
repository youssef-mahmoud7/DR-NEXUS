The source page states that this GLB is free to use, modify, and redistribute for any purpose, including commercially. The asset contains a torso shell and separate meshes for the heart, lungs/airways, major vessels, liver, stomach, bowel, kidneys, and related structures.

It does **not** include a brain, skeletal system, or muscular system. Those systems are shown as unavailable in the DR. NEXUS interface rather than simulated with substitute anatomy.
